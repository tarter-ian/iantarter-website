WizKids · Summers 2024 and 2025

Choosing characters with simulation

I built a model to help WizKids decide which Star Trek characters go into each expansion of Star Trek: Captain's Chair, weighing what fans want against what each license costs.

Role
Research & Development Intern
Methods
Fan sentiment research, scoring, Monte Carlo simulation
Built with
Python, ChatGPT for research
Outcome
Recommended line-ups presented to my manager

The problem

Star Trek: Captain's Chair is a WizKids board game where each player commands a crew from the shows. Every expansion needs its own cast of characters, and WizKids pays for each one through a license.

Licenses are sold in bundles by show. Adding one fan favorite can mean paying for that show's whole bundle, so a character's real cost depends on who else is already in the box.

Which characters should go into which expansion to give fans the most for what the licenses cost?

My approach

First, a measure of what fans want. I used ChatGPT to gather fan opinion about each character from across the web, then turned it into a point score per character.

Then, the cost side. I grouped the characters into their licensing bundles by show, so the model paid for a bundle once, however many of its characters it used.

Then, the search. I wrote a Python model that builds random line-ups across the expansions, scores each one by fan points against licensing cost, and keeps the best. A plain ranking of characters would have worked too, but random search was just as easy to build.

Outcome

I presented the model and its recommended line-ups to my manager. I don't know how much they shaped the final boxes. The game has sold well since.

WizKids also had me score outside game pitches. I built a weighted rubric from the brand and from historical trends in the games market, used it to rank hundreds of pitches, and modeled how different kinds of players would respond to each game.

Next time, I'd test more than one model. I'd also find a sturdier way to measure how well a character or a game fits a product than fan opinion gathered by a chatbot.
