About

I studied cognitive science at Carleton College, with a minor in data science and statistics. I worked in two labs there: one designed studies to help language learners get comfortable making mistakes, and the other measured allyship with researchers at Northwestern, UChicago and Yale.

I also design board games. Button Shy Games is publishing The Daily Weather, which raised over $29,000 from 1,800+ backers on Kickstarter.
