About

I'm finishing Carnegie Mellon's Master of Human-Computer Interaction in August 2027. Before that, I studied Cognitive Science at Carleton College, with a minor in Data Science and Statistics. I worked in three labs there, immersing myself in research about embodied cognition, relationship science, linguistics, and produced several studies and papers in collaboration with researchers from Kyoto University, Northwestern, University of Chicago, Wellesley, and Carleton College.

I have also been designing board games for over a decade. My first public release, The Daily Weather, published by Button Shy Games, raised over $29,000 from 1,800+ backers on Kickstarter.

Currently: Building My Offset, a tool to mitigate environmental harm.
Also collaborating to build products that respond to AI harm, better track airport systems, and mitigate bias in first-time managers.
